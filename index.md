# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Orenda Studios** ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with **FUN SHOOTER VR** ("the App", "the Game", or "FUN SHOOTER VR"), available on Meta Quest and through the Meta Horizon Store.

By using FUN SHOOTER VR, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. About FUN SHOOTER VR

FUN SHOOTER VR is a virtual reality game developed by Orenda Studios for Meta Quest devices.

The Game includes single-player gameplay and multiplayer gameplay. The multiplayer mode supports sessions of up to **5 players**.

FUN SHOOTER VR does not currently provide voice chat between players.

## 2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use and the services enabled in the Game, we may process information necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the Game.

### 2.1 Meta Platform Information

When you use FUN SHOOTER VR through the Meta Horizon platform, Meta may provide or process certain information associated with your Meta account and use of the platform.

This may include platform identifiers and information necessary for features such as entitlement verification, multiplayer functionality, matchmaking, player identity, or other Meta platform services that we have enabled.

We do not request or intentionally collect your Meta account password.

Meta processes information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

### 2.2 Game and Gameplay Information

FUN SHOOTER VR may store or process information associated with your gameplay, such as:

* Player profile information created within the Game.
* Gameplay progression.
* Scores and statistics.
* Experience points, coins, rewards, or other game progression data.
* Game settings and preferences.
* Multiplayer session information.
* Information required to connect players to a multiplayer session.
* Information associated with reports or moderation actions.

This information is used to provide the Game's features and maintain game functionality.

### 2.3 Multiplayer Information

FUN SHOOTER VR supports multiplayer Clay Shooting sessions with up to **5 players**.

If you participate in multiplayer gameplay, information necessary to establish and operate multiplayer sessions may be processed.

This may include player identifiers, lobby or session information, matchmaking information, and gameplay-related information required for players to participate in the same multiplayer experience.

FUN SHOOTER VR does not currently provide player-to-player voice chat.

## 3. User Reports

FUN SHOOTER VR may provide users with mechanisms to report inappropriate behavior or other issues involving other players.

Reports may be processed through Meta's User Reporting Service or other reporting functionality made available through the Meta platform.

Depending on the reporting system used, information contained in a report may include the reason for the report, information about the reported player, information about the reporting player, and other information necessary to investigate or process the report.

We use reports for purposes such as safety, moderation, investigation of violations, and maintaining the integrity of the Game.

## 4. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

FUN SHOOTER VR does not intentionally collect the following information unless a future feature specifically requires it and this Privacy Policy is updated accordingly:

* Precise GPS location.
* Telephone numbers.
* Contacts from your device.
* Photos or videos from your device.
* Microphone recordings or voice communications.
* Camera recordings.
* Payment card information.
* Government identification documents.
* Health information.
* Biometric information for identification purposes.

The Game does not currently provide voice chat and therefore does not intentionally collect or store player voice communications.
